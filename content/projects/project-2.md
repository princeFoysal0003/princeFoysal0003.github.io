### Abstract
The IOT-based Air Quality Monitoring System is an Arduino based project designed to
measure and display real-time air quality,air humidity and temperature,the quantity of air
pollutant such as Carbon Monoxide(CO) in the air.This system is also capable of detecting
LPG gas in case of gas leakage in house.
Poor indoor air quality and potential gas leaks can lead to significant health and safety concerns
in modern households. Traditional methods of monitoring environmental factors such as
temperature, humidity, and gas levels are often inadequate and inconsistent. This system
provides real-time data and instant alerts to a mobile app named IOT Cloud, ensuring users are
always updated, regardless of location. By leveraging low-cost sensors and smart technology,
the project improves household safety and promotes environmental consciousness in addition
with smart home automation.
This project employs an Arduino Uno microcontroller interfaced with an MQ135 gas sensor to
detect various air pollutants, a DHT11 sensor to measure temperature and humidity,an MQ-06
LPG gas sensor to detect LPG gas leakage,an MQ-07 carbon monoxide sensor to measure the
concentration of carbon monoxide in air and an ESP8266 wi-fi module to transmit the data
through wi-fi and the data will be displayed on IOT CLOUD app. The MQ135 sensor provides
analog readings corresponding to the concentration of gases like ammonia, benzene, and
carbon dioxide. These readings are transformed into percentage which indicates the percentage
of good air.The temperature and humidity data is in degree and percentage respectively.The
MQ-06 and MQ-07 sensors data are transformed in Parts Per Million(PPM) [1].
